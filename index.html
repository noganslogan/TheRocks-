THE ROCKS.

You had plans this weekend.
Your wife volunteered you to clear a billion rocks out of your friend's dad's yard. Start with your hands. Upgrade your tools. The truck never fills up.

Move with the left stick. Drag the world to look around. Collect beside the pile, walk to the truck, and unload. Spend rock credits at the shed. Pick up vehicles outside. On desktop, use WASD to move, E or Space to work, and F to enter or exit, and P for your phone.
